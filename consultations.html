Consultations

Updated: August 2026

Consultations are dedicated sessions with InviteDaddy to discuss your specific Schengen, Ireland or UK visa case, confirm the right route, and plan next steps and timeline — bookable as a standalone session or as the starting point before any other InviteDaddy service.

Whether you're an applicant in India, Nepal, Pakistan or Bangladesh who is just starting to explore your travel and visa options, or you're already partway through an application and want a second opinion, a consultation gives you a direct, focused conversation about your specific circumstances before you commit time or money to any next step.

What happens in an InviteDaddy consultation?

A consultation is a focused discussion of your travel purpose, destination(s), timeline, and any documents or decisions you already have. We talk through what you're trying to achieve, what stage you're at, and what realistically applies to your nationality and circumstances. The session ends with clear next steps, so you leave knowing exactly what to do — and in what order — rather than with a general overview.

How do I book a consultation?

You can book a consultation through the Contact page, by phone on +353 87 977 8939, or by email at info@invitedaddy.ie. Let us know your destination and travel purpose when you get in touch, and we'll confirm a time and anything useful to prepare beforehand.

Is the first consultation different from ongoing case support?

The first consultation typically overlaps with a case assessment, confirming the right visa route and requirements for your situation from the outset. Ongoing consultations continue through later stages of your case — for example alongside document preparation or application monitoring — whenever you need to check in, adjust plans, or get a second opinion as things progress.

What should I bring or prepare for a consultation?

It helps to have your intended travel dates and destination, your nationality, a short summary of your travel purpose and travel history, and any documents or decisions already in progress. You don't need a complete picture before booking — bringing what you have is enough for a productive session.

Can I have a consultation if I haven't decided on my destination yet?

Yes — this is a common starting point. Many applicants come to a consultation still weighing up Schengen, Ireland or UK options. We can help clarify which destination and visa route best fits your travel purpose and circumstances before you move any further.

How does a consultation work in practice?

  1. Book a time

    Book a time via the contact page, by phone, or by email.

  2. Share basic details in advance

    If possible, share basic details about your situation ahead of time so the session can be as useful as possible.

  3. Attend the consultation

    Attend the consultation and discuss your case — destination, timeline, documents, and any questions you have.

  4. Receive your next steps

    Receive a clear summary of next steps and any recommended InviteDaddy services for your case.
