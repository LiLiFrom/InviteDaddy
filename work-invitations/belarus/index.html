Work destination · Belarus

Work Placement and Invitations for Belarus — Full Support from InviteDaddy

InviteDaddy places workers from India, Nepal, Pakistan and Bangladesh with employers in Belarus through direct agreements, arranges the official work invitation and supporting documents, and continues to support each worker after arrival — including communication with the employer and assistance with document renewals.

Most agencies stop at the invitation letter. InviteDaddy's Belarus programme runs from finding you a confirmed vacancy through to being the people you call when something goes wrong six months after you land.

What does the Belarus work placement include?

This is a full-cycle service, not a document-preparation service. It covers four things that are usually sold separately, plus one that most agencies do not offer at all:

  • Placement with a real employer. InviteDaddy holds direct agreements with employers in Belarus and matches you to a confirmed vacancy — you are not handed paperwork and told to find work yourself.
  • The official work invitation. Arranged with the employer as part of the placement, in the form the Belarusian authorities require.
  • The full document package. Prepared and checked before you submit your visa or work permit application in your own country.
  • Case monitoring. Tracking the application through to the decision and helping you respond fast if the authorities ask for more.
  • Support after you arrive. Ongoing help with the employer, renewals and disputes — see the section below.

If you are not looking for a job in Belarus and only need help with a Belarusian visa application, that is a different service — see Belarus visa support instead of this page.

Who can apply?

The Belarus programme is open to workers from India, Nepal, Pakistan and Bangladesh who are applying from their country of residence. You do not need to be in Belarus, or in Ireland, to start — the entire placement and preparation process is handled remotely, and you submit your visa or permit application locally.

How does the placement process work?

Six stages, from first contact to established employment in Belarus. The table sets out who does what at each stage, so it is clear where InviteDaddy acts and where you act.

Belarus work placement — division of responsibility by stage
Stage What InviteDaddy does What the worker does
1. Placement Reviews your profile and matches you to a confirmed vacancy with a partner employer in Belarus under an existing direct agreement. Provides work history, qualifications and preferences; confirms acceptance of the offered role and conditions.
2. Invitation Arranges the official work invitation with the employer in the form required by the Belarusian authorities. Supplies accurate passport and personal details for the invitation.
3. Document package Assembles and verifies the full supporting package, flagging anything needing translation, certification or correction. Provides the underlying documents and arranges any certifications required locally.
4. Visa / work permit Completes and cross-checks the application, books the appointment slot, and monitors the case through to decision. Does not submit on the worker's behalf. Submits the application and attends the appointment in person in their own country, as the applicant on record.
5. Arrival Coordinates arrival details with the employer and confirms what to expect in the first days. Travels to Belarus and begins employment with the matched employer.
6. Ongoing support Stays in contact with worker and employer; assists with renewals; mediates if disputes arise. Raises any issue with InviteDaddy as it arises, rather than waiting.

The difference · after arrival

What support is provided after arrival in Belarus?

This is the part most agencies do not offer. Once you have landed and started work, InviteDaddy does not close your file — the relationship continues for as long as you are in the placement.

  • Communication with your employer. Because the placement runs through a direct agreement, InviteDaddy already has a working relationship with the employer and can raise questions about duties, conditions, hours or pay on your behalf.
  • Help with document renewals. Permits and registrations have expiry dates. InviteDaddy helps you track and prepare renewals ahead of the deadline, rather than after a document has lapsed.
  • Mediation in disputes. If a disagreement or misunderstanding arises with the employer, InviteDaddy acts as an intermediary between both sides to resolve it.
  • A point of contact who knows your case. You are not explaining your situation from scratch to a stranger — the same company that arranged the placement handles the follow-up.

Frequently asked questions

Does InviteDaddy find the employer, or do I need to find one myself?

InviteDaddy finds the employer. Placements are arranged through direct agreements with employers in Belarus, so you are matched to a specific confirmed vacancy rather than being given documents and left to search on your own.

What happens after I arrive in Belarus?

Support continues after arrival. InviteDaddy stays in contact with both you and the employer, helps with document renewals as deadlines approach, and acts as an intermediary if a dispute or misunderstanding arises with the employer. This ongoing support is what distinguishes the service from agencies that stop once the invitation is issued.

Does InviteDaddy submit my Belarusian visa or work permit application for me?

No. You submit your visa or work permit application yourself in your country of residence, through the relevant Belarusian embassy, consulate or visa application centre. InviteDaddy arranges the placement and official invitation, prepares and checks the full document package, and monitors the case, but you remain the applicant on record. Approval is never guaranteed, and the decision rests with the Belarusian authorities.

Is this the same as the Belarus visa support service?

No. This page describes employment placement: InviteDaddy matches you to a confirmed vacancy with a partner employer in Belarus, arranges the official work invitation, prepares the document package and continues to support you after arrival. The separate Belarus visa support service is for people who already know why they are travelling and only need help with the visa application itself, with no job placement involved. If that describes you, use the Belarus visa support page instead of this one.

What happens if there is a problem with my employer in Belarus?

Contact InviteDaddy. Because placements are arranged through direct agreements with the employers, InviteDaddy has an existing working relationship with them and can raise issues, mediate disputes and help resolve misunderstandings about conditions, duties or documentation.

Who pays the fees, and what do they cover?

InviteDaddy's service fee is separate from official state charges such as consular fees and permit fees, which are paid to the relevant authorities. All fees are confirmed in writing before any work begins, and no work starts without your agreement.

Please note: Belarusian visa, work permit and employment rules are set by the Belarusian authorities and can change without notice. InviteDaddy is an independent private company, not affiliated with any embassy, consulate or government body, and does not submit applications on a worker's behalf — you apply from your country of residence as the applicant on record. InviteDaddy does not guarantee visa or work permit approval, or any particular employment outcome; final decisions rest with the relevant authority.
