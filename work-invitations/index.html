Work placement abroad

Work Invitations and Placement Abroad

InviteDaddy places workers from India, Nepal, Pakistan and Bangladesh with employers in Serbia, Ukraine and Belarus through direct agreements, arranges the official work invitation and the full document package, and continues to support each worker after arrival — including communication with the employer, assistance with document renewals, and mediation if a dispute arises. This is a full-cycle service, not an invitation letter sold on its own.

Most agencies hand over an invitation and disappear. The value here is what happens after the paperwork: a confirmed vacancy at the start, and someone who knows your case still answering the phone months after you land.

What does full-cycle placement actually mean?

Five things, of which most agencies sell only the middle one:

  • A confirmed vacancy. InviteDaddy holds direct agreements with employers and matches you to a real, specific role — you are not handed documents and told to find work yourself.
  • The official work invitation. Arranged with the employer in the form the destination country's authorities require.
  • The full document package. Prepared and checked for consistency before you submit your own application.
  • Case monitoring. The application tracked through to a decision, with fast help if the authorities ask for more.
  • Support after you arrive. The part below.

The difference · after arrival

What happens after you arrive?

Your file does not close when you land. For as long as you are in the placement, InviteDaddy remains a point of contact who already knows your case and already has a working relationship with your employer.

  • Communication with your employer. Questions about duties, conditions, hours or pay can be raised through InviteDaddy rather than by you alone in a second language.
  • Help with document renewals. Permits and registrations expire. Renewals are prepared ahead of the deadline, not after a document has lapsed.
  • Mediation in disputes. If a disagreement arises with the employer, InviteDaddy acts as an intermediary between both sides.
  • A point of contact who knows your case. The same company that arranged the placement handles the follow-up — no explaining your situation from scratch.

Who submits the visa or work permit application?

You do. You remain the applicant on record and submit your application in your country of residence, attending any appointment in person as required. InviteDaddy arranges the placement and invitation, prepares and cross-checks the document package, books the appointment slot and monitors the case — but does not submit on your behalf and is not affiliated with any embassy or government body.

Do I need a visa service instead?

If your goal is a visit rather than employment — tourism, family, business travel — that is a different service. See Visa Destinations We Cover for Armenia, Georgia, Azerbaijan and Belarus visa support.

Frequently asked questions

Is this a recruitment service or just document preparation?

It is a full-cycle placement service. InviteDaddy holds direct agreements with employers abroad and matches workers to confirmed vacancies, then arranges the official invitation and the full document package. Document preparation is one part of the service, not the whole of it.

What makes InviteDaddy different from other work invitation agencies?

Support continues after arrival. Most agencies close the file once the invitation is issued. InviteDaddy stays in contact with both the worker and the employer, helps with document renewals as deadlines approach, and acts as an intermediary if a dispute arises.

Does InviteDaddy submit my visa or work permit application for me?

No. You submit your visa or work permit application yourself in your country of residence and remain the applicant on record. InviteDaddy arranges the placement and official invitation, prepares and checks the document package, and monitors the case.

Which countries can I be placed in?

InviteDaddy currently arranges work placements in Serbia, Ukraine and Belarus, working with employers in each country under direct agreements.

Is employment or a work permit guaranteed?

No. An official invitation is a required part of the application but does not guarantee approval, and no agency can guarantee an immigration or employment outcome. Decisions rest with the relevant authorities.

Please note: Visa, work permit and employment rules are set by the authorities of each destination country and can change without notice. InviteDaddy is an independent private company, not affiliated with any embassy, consulate or government body, and does not submit applications on a worker's behalf — you apply from your country of residence as the applicant on record. InviteDaddy does not guarantee visa approval, work permit approval, or any employment outcome.
