Armenia Visa from India: Step-by-Step

Indian nationals apply for an Armenian visa in India, through the Armenian diplomatic mission or authorised visa application centre serving their region. InviteDaddy, an Ireland-registered company, prepares the application remotely — confirming the route, checking documents, completing the form, securing the appointment and monitoring the case — while the applicant submits it themselves and remains the applicant on record.

This guide walks through the general process. Specific requirements depend on your visa category and are set by the Armenian authorities, so treat it as a roadmap rather than a final checklist — see Visa Consultation & Case Assessment for a review of your own case.

How do I apply for an Armenian visa from India?

The process runs in six stages: confirm your visa route, secure an appointment, prepare and check your documents, complete the application form, submit it in person, and wait for a decision. InviteDaddy handles everything that can be done in advance so that what you hand over at the counter is complete and consistent.

  1. Confirm your route

    The visa category that applies to your trip and nationality is confirmed first, so you are not gathering paperwork for the wrong category. See Case Assessment.

  2. Book your appointment

    Availability at the centre serving your region is tracked and a slot secured within your travel window. See Appointment Booking Assistance.

  3. Prepare and check documents

    Every document is checked against the requirements for your category, with translation or certification gaps flagged early. See Document Preparation & Verification.

  4. Complete the form

    The application form is completed and cross-checked field by field against your passport and supporting documents. See Form Completion.

  5. Submit in person

    You attend the appointment and submit the application yourself, as the named applicant.

  6. Wait for the decision

    The case is monitored and you are helped to respond quickly if the authorities request more. See Application Monitoring & Support.

Frequently asked questions

How do I apply for an Armenian visa from India?

You apply in India through the Armenian diplomatic mission or authorised visa application centre serving your region, submitting the application form, passport, photo and supporting documents for your travel purpose. InviteDaddy prepares the application remotely from Ireland, but you submit it yourself and remain the applicant on record.

Do Indian citizens need a visa for Armenia?

Whether a visa is required depends on your nationality, travel purpose and intended length of stay. The specific requirement that applies to Indian passport holders is confirmed at the case assessment stage before any documents are prepared.

Can InviteDaddy submit my Armenian visa application for me?

No. You submit the application yourself in India. InviteDaddy checks your documents, completes the form, helps secure your appointment slot and monitors the case afterwards, but it is not a representative of any embassy and does not lodge applications on a client's behalf.

How long does the Armenian visa process take for Indian applicants?

Two timelines apply. InviteDaddy's preparation time depends mostly on how quickly you supply your documents. The official processing time is set by the Armenian authorities and is outside any agency's control. Appointment availability can add to the overall timeline.

Does InviteDaddy guarantee my Armenian visa will be approved?

No. No agency can guarantee a visa outcome. The decision rests entirely with the Armenian authorities. InviteDaddy's role is to make sure the application you submit is complete, accurate and internally consistent.

Please note: Armenian visa rules, fees and processing times are set by the Armenian authorities and can change without notice.
