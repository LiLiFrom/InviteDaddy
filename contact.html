What to include

Giving us these details up front saves a round of questions and lets us answer properly first time:

  • Your nationality and current country of residence
  • The destination you are applying for, or that you are still deciding
  • Your travel purpose — tourism, business, family visit, or employment
  • Your intended dates, even approximate
  • Any previous visa refusal, which materially affects how an application must be prepared
  • Your payment reference, if your question concerns an existing payment

Existing clients

If you have already paid, quote the payment reference from your emailed receipt. Questions about delivery timelines are answered in the Service Delivery Policy; refund questions in the Refund & Cancellation Policy.

Complaints

Email info@invitedaddy.ie with the details and your payment reference. The complaints procedure is set out in section 11 of our Terms & Conditions. If your question is about a card charge, please contact us before your bank — we can usually resolve it faster.
