Application Monitoring & Support

Updated: August 2026

Application Monitoring & Support means InviteDaddy tracks your Schengen, Ireland or UK visa application after submission and helps you respond quickly if the consulate requests additional documents or information, reducing the risk of delay from a missed deadline.

For applicants in India, Nepal, Pakistan, Bangladesh and neighbouring South Asia, the wait between submitting a Schengen, Ireland or UK visa application and receiving a decision can be stressful — especially since a consulate's request for more information often comes with a short response window that is easy to miss if you're not checking regularly. This service exists to keep an eye on your application and make sure nothing is missed.

How can I check my Schengen, Ireland or UK visa application status?

Most Schengen, Ireland and UK visa systems issue an online tracking reference or status portal at the point of submission, so you can check progress directly. The exact mechanism — a website, a portal login, or a reference-number lookup — varies by consulate, visa application centre and authority, so the specific steps depend on where and how you applied. InviteDaddy logs your tracking reference as soon as your application is submitted and checks your status on your behalf on a regular basis, so you don't have to monitor it yourself.

What happens if the consulate asks for more documents after I apply?

It's common for a consulate or visa authority to request additional documents or clarifying information after an application has been submitted, and these requests usually come with a stated deadline for response. Missing that deadline can lead to the application being refused or delayed. InviteDaddy watches for these requests as part of this service, helps you understand exactly what is being asked for, and works with you to prepare and submit a complete response within the given timeframe.

How long does visa processing take for Schengen, Ireland and UK applications?

Under the EU Visa Code, standard Schengen (Type C) visa processing is up to 15 calendar days from the date of submission. This can be extended to up to 30 days where further examination is needed, and in individual cases it can exceptionally take up to 60 days. Ireland and UK visa processing times work differently and vary by visa category — they are published by the relevant visa office, and we recommend checking the current official guidance for your specific application rather than relying on a fixed number, since InviteDaddy does not set or control these timeframes.

What is the step-by-step process InviteDaddy follows?

  1. Submission confirmation & tracking reference logged

    Once your application is submitted, we confirm the submission and log your official tracking reference.

  2. Regular status checks

    We check your application's status on the relevant official tracking channel on a regular basis.

  3. Immediate alert on any request for more information

    If the consulate or authority requests additional documents or information, we make sure you're alerted right away.

  4. Response prepared and submitted within the deadline

    We help you prepare a complete response and get it submitted well within the stated deadline.

  5. Decision received and next steps advised

    Once a decision is issued, we help you understand it and advise on the appropriate next steps.

What should I do if my application is taking longer than expected?

Start by checking your official tracking channel for any status update, or any pending request that may be waiting for a response from you. If there's no update there, contact the visa application centre or consulate directly to ask about your application — they hold the authoritative information on your case. A longer-than-expected wait does not, by itself, mean your application will be refused; processing times can vary for a range of ordinary reasons.

Does InviteDaddy get notified if my consulate reaches out to me?

No. Official correspondence from a consulate or visa authority is sent directly to you, the applicant, as required by each system, and InviteDaddy does not intercept or replace this official communication. What we ask is that you share any request you receive with us as soon as possible, so we can help you prepare and submit a fast, complete response before the deadline passes.
