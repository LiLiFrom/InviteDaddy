Immigration Services

Updated: August 2026

Immigration Services covers broader case support beyond a single visa application — including guidance on longer-stay permissions, status questions and related paperwork for Ireland, the UK and Schengen countries. InviteDaddy provides document and process support, not legal representation; complex legal matters should be referred to a qualified immigration solicitor.

Most applicants from India, Nepal, Pakistan and Bangladesh come to us for a single short-stay visa application. But some situations go further than that — a longer-stay permission, a change in status, a renewal, or a question about what happens after an application is decided. This page covers how InviteDaddy supports those broader cases, and, just as importantly, where our support ends and a qualified immigration solicitor's role begins.

What counts as an "immigration service" versus a standard visa application?

A standard visa application is a defined, short-stay process — you apply for a specific visa category, submit a fixed set of documents, and receive a decision. Broader immigration services cover matters that sit outside that single process: longer-stay permissions, renewals, or status-related questions that involve more complex documentation, multiple stages, or ongoing case management rather than one application and one outcome.

Can InviteDaddy help with long-stay or residency-related paperwork?

InviteDaddy can help organise and prepare documentation and explain general processes for longer-stay matters, within the scope of administrative support. This means we can help you gather and check documents, understand what a given process generally requires, and stay organised as your case moves through it. It does not mean we act on your behalf as legal representatives or interpret the law for you.

Does InviteDaddy provide legal immigration advice?

No. InviteDaddy is not a law firm and does not provide legal representation or formal legal advice. Our role is document preparation, process guidance and administrative support. If your case involves a refusal, an appeal, or a residency or status dispute, these are legal matters, and we recommend consulting a qualified immigration solicitor who can advise and represent you formally.

What should I prepare before an immigration case discussion?

Come to the discussion with a summary of your current status, relevant dates (entry dates, expiry dates, decision dates), any prior applications or decisions relevant to your case, and a clear statement of your goal. The more complete this picture is, the faster we can point you toward the right process — or the right solicitor, if the matter is legally complex.

Is immigration case support different across Ireland, the UK and Schengen countries?

Yes. Each jurisdiction has its own immigration authority, rules and processes. Ireland, the UK, and individual Schengen member states are not interchangeable — a process, timeline or requirement that applies in one does not automatically apply in another, even for similar-sounding situations. Confirming the correct jurisdiction and authority for your case is one of the first things we do.

What is the step-by-step process InviteDaddy follows?

  1. Initial case discussion

    We talk through your situation, your current status and what you're trying to achieve.

  2. Review of current status and documentation

    We review the dates, decisions and documents relevant to your case so far.

  3. Guidance on the relevant process and required paperwork

    We explain the general process that applies to your situation and what documentation it typically requires.

  4. Referral to a qualified solicitor if the matter is legally complex

    If your case involves a refusal, appeal, or a status dispute, we point you toward a qualified immigration solicitor rather than attempting to advise on it ourselves.
