Document Preparation & Verification

Updated: August 2026

Document Preparation & Verification means InviteDaddy checks each required document — proof of funds, travel insurance, accommodation, employment letters and more — against the specific consulate's published checklist before your appointment, so nothing is missing or inconsistent when you apply.

For applicants in India, Nepal, Pakistan and Bangladesh, a single missing or inconsistent document is one of the most common reasons a Schengen, Ireland or UK visa application is delayed or refused. This service exists to catch those issues before you submit, not after.

What documents do Indian, Nepali, Pakistani and Bangladeshi applicants need for Schengen, Ireland or UK visas?

Exact document lists vary by consulate and are published on each embassy or visa application centre's official website, but most Schengen, Ireland and UK visa applications ask for the same broad categories: identity and travel documents (passport, photos, prior visas), proof of financial means (bank statements, income evidence), accommodation and itinerary details, travel insurance meeting the required minimum coverage, and purpose-specific documents such as employment letters, invitation letters or proof of enrolment. InviteDaddy checks your documents against the specific checklist published by the consulate you're applying to, rather than a generic list.

Why do consulates reject applications for document issues?

Consulates reject applications for document issues when a required document is missing, when details are inconsistent across documents (for example, a name or date that doesn't match the passport), when proof of funds or ties to the home country is judged insufficient, or when travel insurance doesn't meet the minimum coverage required. These are process failures that careful preparation can address, rather than outcomes InviteDaddy or any agency can predict or guarantee.

How does InviteDaddy verify my documents before submission?

InviteDaddy reviews each document you provide against the published checklist for your specific consulate and visa category, checks that names, dates and other details are consistent across every document and match your passport, and flags anything missing, expired or likely to need a certified translation. You receive a clear list of what still needs to be corrected or supplied before your appointment.

  1. Collect your documents

    We work with you to gather every document required for your specific visa category and destination, using the checklist published by the relevant consulate.

  2. Match against the consulate's checklist

    Each document is checked off against the exact requirements published by your destination consulate or visa application centre, not a generic template.

  3. Cross-check consistency

    We check that names, dates of birth, addresses and other details match exactly across your passport and every supporting document.

  4. Flag gaps and translation needs

    Anything missing, expired, or likely to require a certified translation or notarisation is flagged clearly, with an explanation of what's needed.

  5. Hand over your final review pack

    You receive a completed, checklist-matched document pack ready for your visa appointment, with any outstanding items clearly listed.

What is the €30,000 minimum travel insurance rule for Schengen visas?

Schengen Area rules require every short-stay (Type C) visa applicant to hold travel medical insurance covering at least €30,000, valid for the whole Schengen Area and for the full length of the planned stay, covering emergency medical treatment and repatriation. A policy that doesn't meet this minimum, or that only covers one Schengen country, does not satisfy the requirement.

Category What it typically includes Why it matters
Identity & Travel Passport, passport-style photos, prior visas or travel history Confirms who you are and your travel history to the consulate
Financial Bank statements, income or employment evidence, proof of funds Shows you can support yourself during the trip
Accommodation & Itinerary Hotel bookings or invitation letter, flight itinerary, day-by-day plan Demonstrates a clear, verifiable travel plan
Insurance & Purpose-specific Travel medical insurance (€30,000 minimum for Schengen), employment or invitation letters Meets mandatory coverage rules and supports your stated purpose of travel

Do document requirements differ by embassy within the same country?

Yes. Even within India, Nepal, Pakistan or Bangladesh, each Schengen member state's consulate or contracted visa application centre can publish its own checklist, and requirements can differ between destinations applying from the same country. Ireland and the UK each run entirely separate visa systems with their own document requirements, distinct from any Schengen state. InviteDaddy confirms the exact checklist for your specific destination and case as part of the case assessment.

Do I need to get documents translated or notarised?

Some consulates require documents that aren't in English (or the relevant language) to be professionally translated and, in some cases, notarised or certified. This requirement varies by consulate and document type, so applicants should confirm what's needed for their specific case. InviteDaddy flags any translation or notarisation requirement during document review, before your appointment.

Please note: Document requirements and checklists are set by the relevant consulates, embassies and immigration authorities and can change without notice. InviteDaddy is an independent private agency, not affiliated with any embassy, consulate or government body, and cannot guarantee visa approval. Final decisions rest with the relevant consulate or immigration authority.
