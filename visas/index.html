Visa Destinations We Cover

InviteDaddy provides visa application support for Armenia, Georgia, Azerbaijan and Belarus, working with applicants from India, Nepal, Pakistan and Bangladesh. Clients apply from their own country through the relevant embassy, consulate or visa centre, while InviteDaddy prepares the application remotely from Ireland — document checks, form completion, appointment booking, itinerary and case monitoring. InviteDaddy does not submit applications on a client's behalf.

Each destination has its own rules, application channel and document expectations. Pick a destination below for the detail, or start with a case assessment if you are not yet sure which route fits your trip.

How does visa support actually work?

The model is the same for every destination. You remain the applicant: you submit your own application in your country of residence and attend the appointment in person where required. InviteDaddy works remotely from Ireland and handles everything that can be prepared in advance, so that what you hand over at the counter is complete, accurate and internally consistent.

Looking for work abroad rather than a visit?

Visa support prepares you for a trip you have already planned. If your goal is employment abroad, that is a different service: InviteDaddy places workers with employers in Serbia, Ukraine and Belarus through direct agreements, arranges the official invitation, and continues supporting the worker after arrival. See Work Invitations and Placement Abroad.

Please note: Visa rules, fees and processing times are set by the relevant authorities and can change without notice. InviteDaddy does not guarantee visa approval — final decisions rest with the relevant authority.
