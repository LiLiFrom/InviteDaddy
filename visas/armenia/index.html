Armenia Visa Support for Applicants from India, Nepal, Pakistan and Bangladesh

InviteDaddy is an Ireland-based visa consultancy that prepares applicants from India, Nepal, Pakistan and Bangladesh for Armenian visa applications. Clients apply from their home country through the relevant embassy, consulate or visa centre, while InviteDaddy handles document preparation, form completion, appointment booking and case monitoring remotely. InviteDaddy does not submit applications on a client's behalf and is not affiliated with any embassy or government body.

How does InviteDaddy support the application?

Support runs across six stages. You stay in your home country for all of them; InviteDaddy works remotely from Ireland and hands you a complete, checked application to submit yourself.

  1. Consultation and case assessment

    We confirm your correct Armenian visa route, your eligibility and a realistic document checklist for your circumstances before any paperwork begins. See Visa Consultation & Case Assessment.

  2. Document check and verification

    We review every supporting document against the requirements for your visa type and flag anything missing, inconsistent, or needing translation or certification. See Document Preparation & Verification.

  3. Application form completion

    We complete your Armenian visa application form and cross-check every field against your passport and supporting documents so nothing contradicts. See Visa Application Form Completion.

  4. Appointment booking

    After you submit, we track the case and help you respond quickly and completely if the authorities request further information. See Application Monitoring & Support.

Frequently asked questions

Does InviteDaddy submit my Armenian visa application for me?

No. You submit your application yourself in your country of residence, through the relevant Armenian embassy, consulate or visa application centre. InviteDaddy works remotely from Ireland to prepare you for that submission — checking documents, completing forms, booking your appointment slot and monitoring the case afterwards.

Can I apply for an Armenian visa from India, Nepal, Pakistan or Bangladesh?

Yes. Applicants apply from their own country through the Armenian diplomatic mission or authorised visa application centre serving their region. The exact channel available in each country depends on Armenia's current representation and application arrangements there.

Does InviteDaddy guarantee that my Armenian visa will be approved?

No. No agency can guarantee a visa outcome. The decision rests entirely with the Armenian authorities handling your application. InviteDaddy's role is to make sure your application is complete, accurate and consistent before you submit it.

What happens if my documents are incomplete?

InviteDaddy checks your documents against the requirements for your visa type before your appointment and flags anything missing, inconsistent or likely to need translation or certification, so gaps are resolved before you submit rather than after.

Do I need to travel to Ireland to work with InviteDaddy?

No. InviteDaddy is based in Ireland and works with applicants entirely remotely by phone and email. You remain in your home country throughout, and you attend your visa appointment locally.

Can InviteDaddy help if I have had a visa refused before?

InviteDaddy can review your circumstances and previous application history as part of a case assessment, and take that history into account when preparing a new application. Previous refusals do not automatically prevent a new application, but they should always be disclosed accurately.

Please note: Armenian visa rules, fees and processing times are set by the Armenian authorities and can change without notice.
