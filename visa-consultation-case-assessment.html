Visa Consultation & Case Assessment

Updated: August 2026

Visa Consultation & Case Assessment is the first step where InviteDaddy reviews your travel purpose, nationality, and travel history to identify the correct visa route among Schengen, Ireland and UK, and gives you a realistic checklist of documents before you start your application.

Applicants from India, Nepal, Pakistan and Bangladesh often plan trips that could touch more than one destination or visa category. Confirming the right route first avoids applying through the wrong channel or with the wrong document set.

What happens during a visa case assessment?

InviteDaddy reviews your intended travel dates, destination(s), travel purpose (tourism, business, family visit, or work-related), nationality and travel history. From this, we confirm which visa type applies — Schengen Type C short-stay, an Ireland short-stay or long-stay (D) visa, or a UK Standard Visitor visa or other relevant category — and outline the document checklist specific to your case.

How do I know whether I need a Schengen, Ireland or UK visa?

It depends on your destination and purpose. The Schengen Area covers most mainland EU countries under one short-stay visa type, but Ireland and the United Kingdom both sit outside the Schengen Area and each require their own separate application — a valid Schengen visa does not grant entry to either. If your trip includes multiple destinations, you may need more than one visa.

What information do I need to provide for an assessment?

  • Intended travel dates and destination country or countries
  • Nationality and passport details
  • A brief description of your travel purpose
  • A summary of previous international travel, if any
  • Any prior visa refusals, if applicable

No original documents are required at the assessment stage — this is a planning conversation, not a submission.

How long does a case assessment take?

A first case assessment is typically covered in a single consultation session. How much preparation is needed afterward depends on how complex your travel history or circumstances are.

Does a case assessment guarantee my visa will be approved?

No. A case assessment identifies the correct route and gives you a realistic document checklist, but the final decision on any visa application always rests with the relevant consulate or immigration authority. InviteDaddy does not and cannot guarantee approval.

What you receive after your assessment

  1. Confirmed visa route

    Clarity on whether you need a Schengen, Ireland or UK visa — or more than one.

  2. Document checklist

    A checklist specific to your nationality, purpose and destination.

  3. Recommended next step

    Guidance on whether to move to appointment booking, document preparation, or another service next.
