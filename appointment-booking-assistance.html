Appointment Booking Assistance

Updated: August 2026

Appointment Booking Assistance means InviteDaddy tracks and helps you secure the earliest realistic available visa appointment slot at the correct application centre — VFS Global, BLS International or TLScontact — for your Schengen, Ireland or UK visa, so you don't lose weeks to fully-booked centres or miss the appointment window for your intended travel dates.

In India, Nepal, Pakistan and Bangladesh, appointment slots at Schengen, Ireland and UK visa application centres are frequently released in small batches and booked within minutes. This service exists to close that gap for applicants who don't have time to refresh a booking portal daily.

How do I book a Schengen visa appointment from India, Nepal, Pakistan or Bangladesh?

You book directly through the visa application centre contracted by the Schengen member state you're applying to in your country — this is usually VFS Global, BLS International or TLScontact, and the centre can differ by destination country even within the same location. The general process is: register an account on the centre's official portal, select the destination consulate and visa category (short-stay Type C), choose from the available appointment slots, and complete the online booking with your passport and contact details. InviteDaddy confirms the correct centre for your case, monitors slot releases, and helps you complete the booking as soon as a suitable slot opens.

Why are Schengen and UK visa appointment slots so hard to get in South Asia?

Demand regularly outpaces the number of daily slots that visa application centres release, particularly around peak travel seasons and public holidays. Centres release new availability on their own internal schedules — sometimes daily, sometimes weekly — and popular consulates can be fully booked within minutes of new slots opening. Applicants checking manually, once or twice a day, often see no availability at all, even when slots did open briefly.

What is the step-by-step process InviteDaddy follows?

  1. Confirm the correct centre and visa category

    We confirm which application centre and visa category applies to your nationality, destination and travel purpose before any booking attempt begins.

  2. Set your booking window

    We agree your ideal travel dates and the earliest/latest acceptable appointment window with you.

  3. Track slot availability

    We monitor the relevant centre's portal for new slot releases within your window.

  4. Secure the appointment

    As soon as a suitable slot opens, we guide you through completing the booking with your own details, as required by the centre.

  5. Confirm and prepare

    We confirm your booking reference and send a checklist of what to bring to the appointment itself.

Which visa application centre do I use — VFS Global, BLS International or TLScontact?

This depends on the destination country and, in many cases, on your specific country of application, since Schengen member states each contract their own centre network per location. A centre used for one Schengen country's applications in India may not be the same centre that country uses in Nepal, Pakistan or Bangladesh. Ireland and the UK run their own separate visa application centre networks, distinct from any Schengen arrangement. Confirming the right centre is one of the first steps in our case assessment.

What happens if I miss my visa appointment?

If you miss a booked appointment, most centres require you to book again from the start, and during busy periods this can mean a significant wait for the next available slot. InviteDaddy sends advance reminders and helps you prepare the required documents ahead of the appointment date to reduce the risk of this happening.

Frequently asked questions

How do I book a Schengen visa appointment from India, Nepal, Pakistan or Bangladesh?

You book a Schengen visa appointment through the visa application centre that the destination Schengen state uses in your country — typically VFS Global, BLS International or TLScontact. You create an account on that centre's booking portal, select the consulate and visa category, and choose an available slot. InviteDaddy tracks slot availability and helps you secure and prepare for the earliest realistic appointment.

Why are Schengen and UK visa appointment slots so hard to get in South Asia?

Demand for visa appointments in India, Nepal, Pakistan and Bangladesh regularly exceeds the daily slots each application centre releases, especially in peak travel seasons. Centres release new slots on their own schedules, and slots can be booked within minutes of release, which is why applicants who check manually often find nothing available.

Does InviteDaddy book the appointment for me?

InviteDaddy tracks appointment availability across the relevant visa application centre for your case and helps you secure a slot as soon as one opens, guiding you through the centre's own booking system. The appointment is booked under your name and details, as required by the visa application centre and consulate.

How far in advance should I book a visa appointment?

Book as early as your travel planning allows. Most Schengen states allow applications up to six months (some up to nine months for certain categories) before travel, and Ireland and UK visa centres have their own lead times. Booking early gives more flexibility if your first-choice slot is unavailable.

Which visa application centre do I use — VFS Global, BLS International or TLScontact?

It depends on which country you are applying to and which country you are applying from, since each Schengen member state contracts a specific centre network per location, and this can differ between India, Nepal, Pakistan and Bangladesh. InviteDaddy confirms the correct centre for your specific destination and nationality as part of the case assessment.

What happens if I miss my visa appointment?

Missing a booked appointment typically means starting the booking process again from the beginning, and in busy periods this can mean a significant wait for the next available slot. InviteDaddy sends reminders and helps you prepare in advance to avoid this.

Please note: Visa appointment systems, application centres and booking rules are set by the relevant consulates and can change without notice. InviteDaddy is an independent private agency, not affiliated with any embassy, consulate or government body, and cannot guarantee appointment availability or visa approval. Final decisions rest with the relevant consulate or immigration authority.
