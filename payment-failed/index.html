Why do payments get declined?

A declined payment almost always comes from the card issuer, not from us. We are not told the specific reason — issuers do not share it with merchants. The usual causes are:

  • The 3-D Secure step was not completed. The one-time code expired, the banking-app approval was missed, or the window was closed too early. This is the most common cause.
  • International or online payments are blocked. Many cards issued in India, Nepal, Pakistan and Bangladesh have foreign or e-commerce transactions disabled by default. Your bank can enable them, often in the mobile app.
  • A limit was reached. A per-transaction, daily, or international spending limit set by your bank.
  • Insufficient available balance once currency conversion is applied.
  • Card details entered incorrectly — number, expiry date or security code.
  • The card has expired or is not valid for online use.
  • A routine fraud check was triggered, for example because the payment is in a foreign currency.

What to do

  1. Check your card details and try once more

    Re-enter the details carefully and complete the 3-D Secure confirmation promptly when your bank prompts you.

  2. Contact your card issuer

    Ask them to authorise international and online payments in euro. They can usually do this immediately, and they can tell you the exact reason for the decline — we cannot.

  3. Try a different Visa or Mastercard

    If another card is available to you, it may go through where the first did not.

  4. Still stuck? Get in touch

    Email info@invitedaddy.ie or call +353 87 977 8939 and we will help arrange an alternative.

If you see a pending charge: a declined payment sometimes leaves a temporary authorisation hold on your account. This is not a charge and is released automatically by your bank, typically within a few business days. If a hold has not cleared, contact your card issuer — and tell us, so we can confirm from our side that no payment was received.
