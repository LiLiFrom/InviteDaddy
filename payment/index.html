Payment

Secure Payment

You are paying Invite Daddy Limited for professional services — the preparation of your documents and the handling of your application. You are not paying for a visa, a work permit, or any decision by an embassy, consulate or immigration authority. All prices are in euro (EUR).

Before you pay — please read

  • You are paying for a service, not for a result. The fee covers document preparation, form completion, appointment booking and case support. It does not buy a visa, a residence permit, or a work permit.
  • The decision belongs solely to the embassy or consulate. Invite Daddy Limited has no influence over that decision, cannot speed it up, and does not guarantee any outcome. An application prepared correctly can still be refused.
  • Consular, visa centre and other third-party fees are not included. You pay those separately and directly to the relevant body. They are outside this payment and are not refundable by us.
  • You submit your own application. You lodge it yourself in your country of residence and remain the applicant on record. Invite Daddy Limited is an independent private company and is not a representative, agent or partner of any embassy, consulate or government body.
  • A refusal is not grounds for a refund of work already performed. Refund rights are set out in full in the Refund Policy below.

Pay securely

Loading secure payment form…

🔒 Your card details are entered directly into a secure form hosted by our acquiring bank. They are never transmitted through, processed by, or stored on invitedaddy.ie. Invite Daddy Limited never sees or holds your full card number, expiry date or security code.

Which cards are accepted?

Visa and Mastercard, issued in any country, including cards issued in India, Nepal, Pakistan and Bangladesh.

Is the payment 3-D Secure?

Yes. Payments are protected by 3-D Secure (Visa Secure and Mastercard Identity Check). After you enter your card details you may be asked to confirm the payment with your bank — usually a one-time code by SMS, or approval in your banking app. This step happens between you and your card issuer; Invite Daddy Limited is not involved in it and does not receive your authentication details.

If the confirmation step fails or times out, the payment will not go through and your card will not be charged. You can simply try again.

What happens after payment?

You will be shown a confirmation page and receive an email receipt. Work begins after payment is confirmed, on the timeline set out in the Service Delivery Policy. If something goes wrong during payment, see Contact — we can be reached by phone and email.
