About InviteDaddy

An Irish-registered visa & immigration company for India, Nepal, Pakistan and Bangladesh

Updated: August 2026

InviteDaddy is the trading name of Invite Daddy Limited, a private company registered in Ireland under Companies Registration Office number 759077, operating from Mullingar, Co. Westmeath. InviteDaddy helps applicants from India, Nepal, Pakistan and Bangladesh with visa applications for destinations including Armenia, Georgia, Azerbaijan and Belarus, and places workers with employers in Serbia, Ukraine and Belarus. It is an independent private company, not affiliated with any government or embassy.

What is Invite Daddy Limited?

Invite Daddy Limited is the registered legal entity behind the InviteDaddy brand. It is incorporated in Ireland under CRO number 759077, with its registered office at 3rd Floor, The Bank Building, Blackhall Place, Mullingar, Co. Westmeath, N91 W3WP, Ireland. The company was established to support applicants from India, Nepal, Pakistan and Bangladesh navigating visa systems abroad, and to place workers with employers under direct agreements.

Who leads InviteDaddy?

InviteDaddy is directed by Sergei Zoshchuk, who serves as Director of Invite Daddy Limited.

What does InviteDaddy actually do?

InviteDaddy runs two distinct lines of work.

Visa support. Eight services covering a visa application end to end: consultation and case assessment, appointment booking assistance, document preparation and verification, application form completion, travel itinerary preparation, application monitoring and support, immigration services, and standalone consultations. Clients apply from their own country; InviteDaddy prepares the case remotely from Ireland and never submits on a client's behalf. Destinations include Armenia, Georgia, Azerbaijan and Belarus — see all visa destinations.

Work placement abroad. A full-cycle service: InviteDaddy places workers with employers in Serbia, Ukraine and Belarus through direct agreements, arranges the official work invitation and document package, and continues supporting each worker after arrival — including communication with the employer, document renewals and mediation in disputes. See Work Invitations and Placement Abroad.

Why is InviteDaddy registered in Ireland?

Ireland is InviteDaddy's home jurisdiction and the base from which the company operates. Irish company registration (CRO 759077) is a matter of public record, searchable through Ireland's Companies Registration Office, and is part of how InviteDaddy aims to operate transparently with applicants from South Asia.

What InviteDaddy does not do

  • InviteDaddy does not issue visas — only the relevant consulate or immigration authority can approve or refuse an application.
  • InviteDaddy does not guarantee visa approval, processing times, or appointment availability.
  • InviteDaddy is not a law firm and does not provide formal legal representation — see Immigration Services for more on this distinction.
  • InviteDaddy does not submit visa applications on an applicant's behalf — you apply in your own country and remain the applicant on record.
  • InviteDaddy is not affiliated with any embassy, consulate, or government body.
